Training & Service Pricing

All training programs are customized to the individual dog and handler. Availability is limited to ensure quality results.

🐾 Private One-on-One Training Sessions

$150 per hour

Personalized, in-person training sessions tailored to you and your dog. These sessions focus on obedience, foundation skills, leash handling, and clear communication between dog and handler.

Ideal for:

  • Basic obedience
  • Puppy foundations
  • Leash manners
  • Owner education and handling skills

🐾 Private One-on-One Behavior Modification

$300 per hour

Intensive, in-person behavior modification sessions designed for dogs with complex behavioral issues. Each session includes a detailed assessment, structured training, and handler education to address the root cause of behavior.

Focus areas include:

  • Reactivity and aggression
  • Fear and anxiety
  • Resource guarding
  • High-drive behavioral challenges

🐾 Board & Train Program

$1,000 per week
4-week minimum commitment

Your dog stays with the trainer for immersive, structured training in a controlled environment. This program provides consistent daily work, routine, and behavior modification tailored to the dog’s specific needs.

Program includes:

  • Daily structured training sessions
  • Behavior modification and obedience work
  • Proper exposure and confidence building
  • Weekly progress updates
  • Owner transition session upon completion

Additional Information

  • All programs are customized to the individual dog
  • Pricing reflects professional, in-person services
  • Availability is limited to ensure quality and results
  • An evaluation may be required prior to acceptance into behavior modification or board & train programs
